Last updated: September 23, 2026

Spirafeed respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information Spirafeed may process, why the information is used, and how users may contact us regarding their data.

Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, Spirafeed may process information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address, when provided
  • Account authentication information
  • One-time verification codes (OTP) used for account verification
  • Technical information required to protect accounts from abuse and unauthorized access

How We Use Information

Information is processed only as necessary to operate Spirafeed features, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Verifying phone numbers
  • Authenticating users
  • Sending account verification messages
  • Preventing abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of the service

WhatsApp Messages

Spirafeed may use WhatsApp to send account-related messages such as verification codes. WhatsApp messaging is provided through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta.

Spirafeed does not use verification messages for advertising or unrelated marketing purposes.

Passwords and Verification Codes

Passwords are not stored in plain text. Verification codes are used only for verification purposes and are designed to expire after a limited period of time.

Data Sharing

Spirafeed may use service providers when necessary to operate the service, such as messaging infrastructure used to deliver verification messages. Information is shared only when needed to provide the relevant service.

Spirafeed does not sell personal information.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, security requirements, and applicable operational or legal requirements.
